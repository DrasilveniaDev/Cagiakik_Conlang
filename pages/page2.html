The pronouns

An example of a pronouns character on this conlang


The pronouns is a nameless way to call an individual or many individuals. It's commonly used and existent on every language, but on Cagiakik conlang has more details and characteristics that will be helpful for everyone.

The basic descriptions of the pronouns are: Cantity, Selection, Gender and Case; it also has advances descriptions that tells of which class of something is refering, and that extra description goes before the pronouns. This list shows how those descriptions can be used or described for each type:

  • Cantities: Singular (1), Dual (2), Plural (more than 2)
  • Selections: First Person (I, We), Second Person (You), Third Person (He, She, It, They)
  • Genders: Neutral/Agender, Male, Female, Intersexual/Bigender
  • Cases: Nominative, Possesive, Objective & Descriptive

Each characteristic and description that forms a pronouns makes a different combination and word on Cagiakik language. And each characteristic writes a part of the Pronouns character different.
