Communication System

The Fdattag language has 2 communication systems: Oral and written. This language uses Consonants, vowels and tones that I will talk more soon about.


Oral communication

This language has 30 Consonants and 9 Vowels. On the consonants, exists defined consonants and undefined consonants. You must use the defined consonants on this dictionary because they have meanings and are included on the dictionary.

Defined Consonants: Consonants that have a meaning and have a tree that has other more defined words. You can see those trees on the One Syllable Dictionary.
Undefined Consonants: The other consonants that does not count with a meaning nor a tree of words. This is because it hasn't a clear or unknown topic tree specified for an consonant, In summary, they have no defined use.

This language has 17 Defined Consonants and it needs to define the rest of the consonants that are not defined. The Consonants are 56,7% finished.

For finalize, the Fdattag language has 6 tones like Cantonese, however, they're optional for use. No tones means that it's a Sustantive or an Adjective. Hight tones are used for verbs and the low static tone is used for the Pronouns. In latin scripture, no tones means not write a number after a syllable.

Low Hight
1 (Low tone) = Pronouns 2 (High tone) = Present or Infinitive Verb
3 (Low ascend) = Undefined 4 (High ascend) = Future Verb
5 (Low descend) = Undefined 6 (High descend) = Past Verb
