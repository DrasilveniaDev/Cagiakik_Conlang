The Chinese language is the only language that doesn't have this problem. Each character, on chinese has one only syllable, and they use multiple characters for define an more detailled word or thing.

The next problem is that Human languages has homophones and homographs. This can missunderstand a bit the oral communication, but in often cases, the written communication. And finally, Too many languages. The difference between languages can be small or very notorious depending of the family languages. It also made few languages have a low accesibility to translation.

Fdattag conlang solves this problem forming trees of groups and words. All this meaning simplified in one syllable. It also uses tones for verbs or sustantives. Multiple syllables makes specific and detailled words and things. Each syllable means one thing, and All synonymous will be reduced into 1 word on Fdattag conlang.
On this conlang, there are no notorious homophones and homographs, that alter the meaning. Every word on this conlang will be different to each other.
